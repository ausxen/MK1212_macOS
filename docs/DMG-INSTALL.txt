ausxen's MK1212 macOS Launcher

Drag the "MK1212 Mac Launcher" folder onto the Applications shortcut in this
disk image. The installed folder will be:

    /Applications/MK1212 Mac Launcher/

Open "MK1212 Mac Launcher.app" from that folder, or add that app to Steam as
a non-Steam game. To remove the launcher, close ATTILA and open
"Uninstall MK1212 Mac Launcher.app".
