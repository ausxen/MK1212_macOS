ausxen's MK1212 macOS Launcher

FIRST LAUNCH — READ THIS BEFORE INSTALLING
==========================================

1. Verify the SHA-256 checksum of the DMG you downloaded. The matching
   `.dmg.sha256` file is in the repository's `dist/` folder.

2. Open the DMG and double-click `Install MK1212 Mac Launcher.pkg`.

3. Follow the macOS Installer prompts. The launcher will be installed in:

       /Applications/MK1212 Mac Launcher/

4. Open `MK1212 Mac Launcher.app` from that folder.

The embedded apps are ad-hoc signed, and this community package is not
Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks the installer, right-click it, choose Open,
and confirm the user-initiated launch. You can also use Privacy & Security >
Open Anyway after attempting to open it. Do not bypass macOS security prompts
for an unverified copy; verify the release checksum first.
